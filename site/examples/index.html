One board, every feature

A checkout relaunch, start to finish, in one file: example.diagram. The top board is the system; four canvases inside it hold the discovery, the plan, the data and the numbers. Open it in the playground to click through the canvases, or in your terminal with diagram example.diagram.

The system

All thirteen shapes, colours, details, tags, web and in-board links, nested sections, every kind of arrow, review comments with replies, and a theme of its own. The Inside this board section holds the links into the canvases — a button, a card, a text link and a sticky.

Discovery

A prompt written as a mindmap (Context, Goal, Constraints, Steps, Acceptance — "Copy as prompt" turns it into Markdown for a coding agent), a retro as a top-down mindmap, a pasted wireframe, and a canvas of interview notes inside this canvas.

Delivery plan

A kanban board, two gantt charts (one waits for the other's milestone), a risk matrix, decision records with their status, and tables.

Data & flows

An ER model with crow's-foot cardinality and named relationships, the payment as a sequence diagram, and order-to-cash in swimlanes.

Metrics

Every chart type — bars, stacked and horizontal bars, lines, areas, pie, donut and scatter — drawn from small tables, on the paper theme.
